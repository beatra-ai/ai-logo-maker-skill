# Next step after a logo

Follow [offering one next step](next-step-offers.md). A logo counts as
accepted only once the user picks one of the delivered versions; the step
builds on that one. The logo variants in [review and
recovery](review-and-recovery.md) count as a revision suggestion here: when
the user's primary use needs a variant, give that advice instead, and do not
offer a next step in the same message.

Check these in order and offer the first that fits; offer nothing when none
fits.

1. **A sound for the brand, in another Skill.** The user wants sound or music
   for their ads, store, livestream room, or radio spot, or wants a tune or a
   sound logo for the brand. Point to Brand Jingle Studio: the brand name and slogan become
   original jingles and a short sound logo. They prepare the brand name, a
   slogan, and where it will play. Install message, in the user's language:

   ```text
   请安装 广告歌生成·品牌主题曲；需要登录授权时告诉我：https://beatra.ai/skills/brand-jingle-studio/install.md
   Install Brand Jingle Studio and tell me when I need to sign in: https://beatra.ai/skills/brand-jingle-studio/install.md
   ```

2. **The founder telling the brand story, in another Skill.** The user is
   starting or launching the business and wants to introduce the brand on
   camera themselves. Point to Founder IP Avatar Studio: one authorized photo
   and a short script become a talking video in the user's own voice. They
   prepare a front-facing photo and a short voice sample. Install message, in
   the user's language:

   ```text
   请安装 数字人分身·创始人口播；需要登录授权时告诉我：https://beatra.ai/skills/founder-ip-avatar-studio/install.md
   Install Founder IP Avatar Studio and tell me when I need to sign in: https://beatra.ai/skills/founder-ip-avatar-studio/install.md
   ```

3. **A motion logo intro, in place (the usual step).** Offer a 5-second
   animated version of the logo for the start of a video, a store screen, or
   a livestream, per [animating a delivered image](animate-a-delivered-image.md).
   The mark stays still and sharp; the motion is a light sweep, a slow push,
   or a soft glow or particles around it.

A mark used only on paper, uniforms, seals, or stamps, or a wordmark with no
symbol, gets no offer: the first has no screen to play on, and motion bends
lettering in the second.
